Volumetric weight is the parcel’s weight as per its dimensions. Its formula is (L x B x H) / 5000.

The shipment charges are calculated on the basis of volumetric or actual weight, whichever is higher. That’s why it is important to measure your parcel to avoid any surprise charges

Courier companies typically levy a 10 to 12% of fuel surcharge on the base price.

13. What is the difference between air and surface shipping?

Air Express is the quickest option, delivering high-value, time-sensitive orders within 1 to 2 days. Surface shipping ships the parcels via road and takes approximately 2 to 4 days for delivery for competitive pricing and non-urgent orders.
